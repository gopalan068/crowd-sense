CrowdSense acts as a coordination layer over physical safety infrastructure — reducing inflow, creating space, guiding crowds toward available exits, and coordinating police and medical teams. It does not replace responders on the ground.

How the Dynamic Evacuation Pipeline Works

From raw signal to physical response, in seconds
1
CCTV / night vision / drone

Continuously observe where people are, how dense each zone is, and which direction they're moving.

2
IoT sensors

Add environmental conditions and, where applicable, occupancy and density data per zone.

3
Command & Control Centre

CrowdSense combines all sources and identifies the zone that's becoming high-risk.

4
Risk Engine

Determines which zone is dangerous, which exits are available, and which route is safest right now.

5
Digital arrow boards + LED/VMS

The displayed direction updates automatically.

EXIT A → ✕ BLOCKEDEXIT B → USE THIS ROUTE →
6
PA speakers

Give matching voice instructions: "Please move calmly towards Exit B. Do not move towards Zone A."

7
Smart barricades / temporary barriers

Physically redirect the flow and keep people out of the dangerous zone.

8
Access-control barriers

Stop new people from entering an already overloaded zone.

9
Dedicated emergency corridor

Keeps a clear path open for police, medical teams, fire services and ambulances.

10
Smart lighting

Illuminates the currently selected evacuation path, especially in low-light conditions.

11
Emergency exits

The actual physical escape points that the system directs people towards.

12
Assembly points

Once clear of danger, people are guided to predefined safe assembly areas rather than forming a new crowd near the exit.
