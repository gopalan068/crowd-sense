## 2. Spatial Anatomy & Environmental Choke Points

### A. The South Broadway Arterial Corridor (Zone 2 & Zone 7 Interspace)
* **Dimensions:** Width varies from $15.9\text{ m}$ in the south to $9.8\text{ m}$ near the temple approach (Total Broadway span: $\approx 28.1\text{ m}$).
* **Environmental Function:** Primary crowd ingress artery from transit arrival points.
* **Structural Bottleneck:** As the corridor approaches the Temple Compound, the street narrows by 38%, creating a natural funnel. Unmetered inflows from the South will cause density to surge from LOS B ($0.6\text{ p/m}^2$) to LOS E ($2.5\text{ p/m}^2$) within 85 meters.
* **Mitigation:** The **Longitudinal Broadway Barricade** splits this corridor into bidirectional channels, preventing counter-flow collision.

### B. The Procession Chariot Obstacle (Rath) & Gopuram Landmark
* **Dimensions:** Rath footprint is $3.89\text{ m} \times 2.62\text{ m}$ placed centrally in the procession plaza ($X: 323\text{–}375\text{ px}, Y: 230\text{–}265\text{ px}$).
* **Gopuram Tower:** High masonry gateway creating a $7.03\text{ m}$ portal restriction.
* **Risk Factor:** The Chariot creates an aerodynamic drag on human flow. Crowds maneuvering around the stationary Rath experience lateral compression on the West flank against the compound wall.

### C. The North-West Egress Flank (Zone 1 & Zone 3 Interspace)
* **Dimensions:** Corridor width $9.0\text{ m}$ connecting directly to **Exit 1 (West)** ($4.94\text{ m}$ clearance).
* **Risk Factor:** Exit 1 has a right-angle turn into the discharge street. Crowds attempting rapid egress form turbulent diagonal arching.

## 3. Crowd Circulation & Directional Routing Plan

To guarantee safe movement, the venue must operate under a **Strict Unidirectional Loop Principle**:

```
[ South Broadway Entry ]
         │
         ▼
[ Ingress Chute (East Side of Barricade) ]
         │
         ▼
[ Gopuram Darshan Plaza ] ───(Overcrowding)───► [ Emergency Gate 2: East Bypass ]
         │                                                      │
         ▼                                                      ▼
[ Rath Circumambulation Loop ]                          [ North Egress Boulevard ]
         │                                                      │
         ▼                                                      ▼
[ West Corridor Descent ]                               [ Exit 2: North ]
         │
         ▼
[ Exit 1: West ]
```

1. **Ingress Rule:** Attendees entering from South Broadway are channeled exclusively along the **East side** of the longitudinal barricade.
2. **One-Way Circulation:** No attendee may backtrack south along the primary channel. Flow must loop around the Rath toward Exit 1 (West) or continue north to Exit 2.
3. **Barricade Segregation:**
   - **Main Longitudinal Barricade:** Keeps ingress crowd separated from returning egress stream.
   - **Temple NW Flank Barricade:** Prevents incoming crowds from cutting through emergency gate zones.

## 4. Phase-by-Phase Event Execution Protocol

### Phase 0: Pre-Event Setup & Verification (T-4 Hours to T-0)
1. **Clearance Sweep:** Ensure all corridors are completely free of parked two-wheelers, informal vendor carts, and temporary electrical wiring.
2. **Gate Calibration:** Verify both Emergency Gates (Gate 1 & Gate 2) swing outward smoothly and have dedicated marshals equipped with bolt cutters and secondary radios.
3. **Medical Staging:** First-aid post must be anchored in the open pocket north of Zone 6 (East Island), **not** in the narrow South corridor.

### Phase 1: Inflow Metering & Ingress Phase (T+0 to T+2 Hours)
* **Inflow Target:** Maximum **60 persons/minute** per operational entry gate.
* **Metering Control:** If queue length on South Broadway exceeds 45 meters, deploy **Holding Pen Zig-Zag Barricades** at the street mouth to slow approach velocity.
* **Heat Management:** If ambient temperature exceeds $32^\circ\text{C}$, activate misting fans and deploy volunteer water hydration teams along the South Broadway queue.

### Phase 2: Procession & Peak Congregation (T+2 to T+4 Hours)
* **Chariot Movement:** Moving the chariot requires a dedicated $3\text{ m}$ clearance bubble maintained by 12 marshals holding perimeter rope lines.
* **Density Surveillance:** Monitor the corridor between the Rath and the Gopuram. If local density hits **$2.5\text{ p/m}^2$ (Orange Alert)**:
  - Immediately pause further admissions at South Entry.
  - Open **Emergency Gate 2** to vent 35% of the crowd into the eastern bypass corridor.

### Phase 3: Egress & Dispersal Protocol (T+4 Hours Onwards)
* **Exit 1 & Exit 2 Open Full Bore:** Ensure gate leaves are pinned fully open against boundary walls.
* **Public Address Guidance:** Continuous announcements directing crowds: *"Keep moving forward; take Exit 2 North for shuttle buses, Exit 1 West for railway links."*
* **Reverse Clearance:** Sweeper teams move from South to North, ensuring no stranded individuals or children are trapped behind barricade joints.
